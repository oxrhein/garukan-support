Garukan

Account access support for messaging users.

Garukan is a small, transactional support service. It exists for people who are locked out of their own messaging account because the login code never reaches their phone.

What the service does

  1. A user who cannot sign in submits their own phone number to our support bot.
  2. Our system sends one support email to the published support mailbox of the platform the user is trying to access, asking the platform to reopen the login route for that account.
  3. That is the whole transaction. Nothing is stored beyond the request itself.

What the service is not

  • No marketing, newsletters or promotional mail of any kind.
  • No purchased, rented or scraped recipient lists.
  • No bulk or unsolicited sending. Recipients are support addresses published by the platforms themselves; we never choose or discover them on our own.

Bounces and complaints

Delivery feedback is monitored through Amazon SES notifications. Any address that bounces or registers a complaint is suppressed immediately, and sending pauses while the cause is reviewed. Volume is deliberately low: one message per user request, from a dedicated sender address on this domain.
